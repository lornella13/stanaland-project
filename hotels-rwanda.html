HOTEL DES MILLE COLLINES Premium Hotel

HOTEL DES MILLE COLLINES

Hôtel des Mille Collines is a historic four-star luxury hotel located in the heart of Kigali’s central business district. Inaugurated in 1973, it is globally renowned for its real-life historical significance, having sheltered over 1,200 people during the 1994 genocide. Today, the property seamlessly blends its deep legacy with modern, upscale hospitality.
